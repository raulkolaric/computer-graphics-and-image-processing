# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This is a learning project. Beginners ask basic questions here, and we want them to feel safe doing so.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person. Be specific and kind.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and learn from mistakes, yours and others'.
- Credit other people's work.
- Respect differences in opinion, skill, and background.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or demeaning comments, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Passing off someone else's work as your own.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, and any other channel used to discuss the project. It also applies when someone is clearly representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code, and will explain why when appropriate.

**To report a problem,** contact the maintainer, [@raulkolaric](https://github.com/raulkolaric), through the contact options on their GitHub profile. If the concern is about the maintainer, contact another listed author through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential as far as possible. Please include what happened, where, and who was involved.

Maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly.

### Consequences

Maintainers decide proportionate responses, generally in this order:

1. **Correction.** A private note explaining what was inappropriate and why. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A formal warning, with a period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** A time-limited ban from project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban.** Removal from project spaces, for a pattern of violations or a single severe one.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code in good faith may face consequences decided by the other maintainers.
